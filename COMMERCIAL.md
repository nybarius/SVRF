# Commercial and enterprise licensing

SVRF is source-available under the [PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## What the PolyForm Shield license already allows, free

- Using SVRF to run the merge train for your own repositories — personal, open-source,
  or inside a company, including for commercial work.
- Reading, modifying and redistributing the code, as long as the license and the
  `Required Notice:` line travel with it.

## What needs a commercial license

- Offering SVRF, or a product built from it, **in competition with SVRF** — for example
  a hosted merge-queue service, or bundling it into a CI/CD or developer-tools product
  you sell. (See the license's *Noncompete* and *Competition* sections for the exact terms.)
- Any use where your organisation wants terms beyond the PolyForm Shield license:
  warranty, indemnity, support, priority fixes, private features, or a different
  license for procurement reasons.

## Enterprise terms

Commercial and enterprise licenses are negotiated per organisation and can include:

- a license to build competing or embedded products,
- support and response-time commitments,
- help deploying SVRF on your runners and gates,
- warranty and indemnity terms.

## How to ask

Open an issue at <https://github.com/nybarius/SVRF/issues/new> titled
**"Commercial license"** (it can be brief — who you are and what you want to do), and the
maintainer will follow up privately.
